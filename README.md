<h1>Features</h1>
<h3>Active Trains</h3>
<h4>The active trains page shows you all of the Active trains on the Amtrak network. It does not show Amtrak Busses. At the top, you will see the "Go to Random Train" button, this picks a random active train and opens it in the Train Information page. Below that, you will see a huge table featuring the train Number, Name, Origin, Destination and Current Location at a glace. You can click the train number of any train to open it in the Train Information page.</h4>
<h3>Simple Train Information</h3>
<h4>The Simple train information page shows the Origin, Destination, Arrival Time, Departure Time, Number and more of the train you decide to search.</h4>
<h3>Detailed Train Information</h3>
<h4>The Detailed Train Information page shows the same stuff as the simple page but even more, it shows the Station name, Scheduled Arrival & Depature, Actual/Estimated Arrival & Departure, Station Type (eg. Platform With Shelter), Time Zone and More. It is great for people who want all of the Details.</h4>
